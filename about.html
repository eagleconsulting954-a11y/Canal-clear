The Problem

Paperwork errors shut ships down.

The Panama Canal and Suez Canal together handle ~25,000 transits per year. Each requires precise, deadline-driven compliance filings. One wrong field, one missing document, one late submission — and a vessel loses its transit slot.

⏰

Tight Deadlines

Panama VUMPA requires 96-hour advance filing. Suez SCNT bookings require complete documentation before convoy assignment. There is no "almost on time."

📋

Manual, Error-Prone Process

Most operators rely on expensive ship agents who fill out forms manually — often from memory or templates that don't reflect the latest ACP/SCA regulatory updates.

💸

Costly Delays

A missed transit slot cascades into daily demurrage, cargo delivery failures, charter party penalties, and reputational damage.

$2.5K–$50K+ per incident
The Solution

AI-powered compliance, pre-submission validation.

CanalClear replaces the manual filing process with an AI-powered engine that knows canal authority requirements cold — and validates every form field before it ever reaches the portal.

The result: dramatically fewer preventable rejections and missed slots from paperwork.
