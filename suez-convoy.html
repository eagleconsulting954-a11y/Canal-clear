⚓ Suez Bay
Northbound Staging
Position 29°56'N 32°34'E
Capacity ~25 vessels
Type Sheltered bay

Northbound vessels stage here before convoy. Pilot boards at this anchorage. Generally calm — sheltered from Red Sea swells.
⚠️
Pre-Arrival Report: All vessels must report to the relevant VTS (Port Said or Suez Traffic) on Ch 74 upon entering Egyptian territorial waters, minimum 24 hours before arrival at the waiting anchorage. Failure to report may delay convoy assignment.

SCA Vessel Priority Classification

The Suez Canal Authority assigns convoy slots based on vessel type. Priority determines both slot allocation and estimated waiting time at the anchorage.

PRIORITY 1 — HIGHEST
LNG & LPG Carriers
Liquefied natural gas / liquefied petroleum gas carriers
12–36h
Typical waiting time
  • Dedicated pilot and tug assignment
  • Convoy slot guaranteed within 24h of arrival
  • Hazmat papers (DG declaration + SCA/ENRRA permit) due 72h advance
  • Wide-beam LNG carriers require SCA Operations pre-clearance
PRIORITY 2 — HIGH
Tankers, Container & Passenger
Crude/product/chemical tankers · Container ships · Cruise/passenger · RoRo
24–72h
Typical waiting time
  • Tankers: single-hull vessels face additional inspection
  • Container ships: slot priority scales with TEU capacity
  • RoRo: lane dimensions must match SCA pre-arrival notification
  • Cruise ships: passenger manifest to SCA Port Said 48h ahead
STANDARD PRIORITY
Bulk Carriers & General Cargo
Bulk carriers (dry/OBO) · General cargo · Other vessel types
2–5 days
Typical waiting time
  • Bulk carriers in ballast: 75% toll rebate available
  • Bulk carriers laden: 35% rebate program
  • Second northbound convoy often available when first is full
  • Oversize vessels (LOA >300m or beam >60m) need SCA pre-clearance
