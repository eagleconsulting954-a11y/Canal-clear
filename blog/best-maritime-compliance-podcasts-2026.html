Frequently Asked Questions

What podcasts cover Panama Canal compliance?

Clear Passage by CanalClear is the most focused podcast for Panama Canal compliance, covering VUMPA filing, PCSOPEP requirements, crew credentials, and ACP portal navigation. Episodes average 13 minutes and are designed for ship operators who need practical, actionable guidance between transits.

Are there free maritime compliance podcasts?

Yes. All podcasts listed here are free to stream. Most are available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, and Google Podcasts at no cost. Some podcast producers offer premium feed tiers with bonus content, but the core episodes are freely accessible.

How often should ship operators listen to compliance podcasts?

Ship operators should subscribe to compliance podcasts and listen to new episodes as they publish. The Panama Canal ACP updates its Notice to Shipping quarterly, and compliance requirements change with it. Even 15 minutes per week of focused listening keeps operators current on regulation changes, procedural updates, and common compliance mistakes to avoid.

Spotify is the most widely used podcast platform among maritime professionals, followed by Apple Podcasts. YouTube is growing for operators who prefer video.
