FAQ

Common questions

What AI model generates the articles? ▾
We use Claude (Anthropic) for article generation — one of the most capable models for long-form, factually grounded writing. This produces higher-quality output than most SEO tools using older GPT models.
Are the articles really publish-ready? ▾
They're 85–95% ready in most cases. You'll want to add your own examples, brand voice, and any internal links. The AI handles structure, keyword placement, meta, and bulk of the writing — your editor handles polish.
What is AI visibility and why does it matter? ▾
AI visibility measures how often your brand appears when AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) answer questions in your category. With AI handling ~30% of search queries now, this is a critical metric alongside traditional rankings.
How does the backlink tracker work? ▾
You manually add backlinks you've earned or built, including source URL, anchor text, and domain authority. You can then track status changes (active, pending, lost) over time. We're building automated monitoring for a future update.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▾
Yes — cancel from your settings page anytime. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No lock-ins, no cancellation fees.
Do unused article credits roll over? ▾
Article credits reset each billing month and do not roll over. We recommend using them throughout the month as part of a consistent publishing schedule for best SEO results.
