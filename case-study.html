Frequently Asked Questions

What does a single compliance error cost a fleet operator at the Panama Canal?
The total cost of a single compliance incident includes: direct ACP fines, transit slot forfeiture requiring 72+ hours to rebook, vessel operating costs during the wait ($30K–$80K+/day depending on vessel class), cargo penalties, and downstream demurrage at the destination port. The combination cascades quickly. A single error caught by CanalClear before transit prevents the entire sequence.
How does CanalClear reduce filing errors compared to manual processes?
Manual VUMPA and PCSOPEP filings carry high first-submission rejection rates due to complex forms, language barriers, and time pressure — most from preventable data entry errors. CanalClear's AI validation engine flags errors in real-time. The system validates all required fields against ACP's known rejection criteria, cross-checks certificates, and flags missing or expired documentation before submission.
How much time does CanalClear save per transit?
Manual filing with a ship agent takes 2–4 hours of back-and-forth coordination per transit. CanalClear automates the entire workflow — AI document extraction, compliance validation, and PDF generation complete in under 5 minutes. Fleet operators with 10+ transits per month reclaim 20–35+ hours of staff time every month.
What is the ROI of switching from ship agents to CanalClear?
For a fleet of 5 vessels averaging 10 transits per month at $5,000/agent per transit, annual agent costs reach $600,000. CanalClear Agent Pro at $599/month plus Ops Manager at $399/vessel/month for 5 vessels costs $31,140/year — a gross annual saving of $568,860 before accounting for prevented fines. Each prevented compliance incident ($173K–$431K) multiplies the return. Most fleets see payback within the first week.
Does CanalClear support multi-vessel fleet management?
Yes. CanalClear supports up to 5 vessels on the Pro plan and up to 25 vessels on the Fleet plan. Each vessel maintains its own compliance history, PCSOPEP documentation, and transit records — all accessible from a single dashboard.
