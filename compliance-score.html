Frequently Asked Questions

What does the compliance score measure?

The score (0–100) assesses four areas: VUMPA readiness, PCSOPEP requirements based on gross tonnage, documentation completeness, and regulatory risk from flag state and vessel type. Learn more in the full compliance guide →

Does my vessel need PCSOPEP?

PCSOPEP is mandatory for vessels carrying 400+ metric tons of persistent oil as fuel or cargo. Below 400 MT, no PCSOPEP is required. Enter your gross tonnage above to check your specific situation.

How early must VUMPA be filed?

VUMPA must be filed at least 96 hours before arrival at the Canal anchorage (Balboa or Cristóbal). Late filing forfeits your transit slot with no grace period — costing 5–10 day delays and $50K–$150K/day in vessel costs.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no credit card. For a full automated VUMPA filing and compliance validation, see CanalClear's paid plans — which replace $2,000–$5,000+ ship agent fees per transit.

What cargo types require special ACP documentation?

Hazardous materials (IMDG classes), lithium batteries, livestock, and oversized cargo all require additional documentation beyond standard VUMPA. Lithium battery rules have been tightened significantly since 2024.

Can I share this score with my team or ship agent?

Yes. After calculating your score, click "Share this report" to copy a unique link with your vessel's IMO number pre-filled. Results are shareable and accessible on any device.
