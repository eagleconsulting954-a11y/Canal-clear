Methodology

The $65,000 cost-per-incident baseline anchors on the ACP fine schedule (Panama Canal Authority) combined with average demurrage at the canal's $30K–$80K/day rate, typically triggered by documentation rejection and re-filing delays of 0.5–1 day.

Cargo multipliers reflect Panama Canal-specific surcharges: LNG vessels carry higher PCSOPEP obligations and cargo declaration complexity; tankers carry pollutant-certificate scrutiny; bulk/general cargo track near baseline.

Rejection rates are segmented by filing method based on operator-reported data and AMP (Autoridad Marítima de Panamá) published rejection statistics. Mixed-method operations carry the highest rate due to inconsistent handoff documentation.
Sources: ACP Administrative Notice PAN-2024-17, AMP Fine Schedule 2025, MarineTraffic delay data
