FAQ

Everything you need to know before you start

What is CanalClear?+
CanalClear is an AI-powered maritime compliance automation platform covering the Panama Canal (VUMPA, PCSOPEP), Suez Canal (SCA forms, SCNT, ISPS), Bosporus/Turkish Straits (SP-1, Montreux Convention), Malacca Strait (STRAITREP, VTIS), and Cape of Good Hope (SAMSA ISPS pre-arrival). It replaces expensive ship agent fees ($2,000–$5,000+ per transit) by automating the entire compliance documentation workflow across all five critical maritime chokepoints.
Which waterways and filing types are covered?+
All five critical chokepoints: Panama Canal (VUMPA, PCSOPEP, crew manifests), Suez Canal (12+ SCA forms, SCNT tonnage, ISPS scheduling, convoy booking), Bosporus/Turkish Straits (SP-1 pre-arrival, Montreux Convention checks, dangerous cargo alerts), Malacca Strait (STRAITREP, VTIS compliance, MARPOL validation — 19-check automation), and Cape of Good Hope (SAMSA ISPS pre-arrival, Marine Notice 12 of 2008, 96-hour deadline tracking).
How much can I save vs using a ship agent?+
Traditional ship agents charge $2,000–$5,000+ per transit for compliance documentation. CanalClear's Ops Manager plan starts at $349/vessel/month — covering unlimited filings for your chosen waterway. A fleet of 10 vessels transiting Panama twice a month at $2,500/agent fee spends $600K/year on agents alone. CanalClear replaces that with a flat subscription, typically recovering the cost on the very first avoided agent fee.
How does the AI validation work?+
Before any filing reaches the authority portal, CanalClear's AI checks every field against the authority's current requirements — ACP for Panama, SCA for Suez, VTSC for Bosporus, MPA for Malacca, SAMSA for Cape. It validates vessel particulars, cargo declarations, crew data, ISPS levels, and certificate expiry dates. Errors that would cause a rejection (and forfeit your transit slot) are flagged before submission so you can fix them in time.
Is there a free trial? How do I get started?+
Yes — you can try a live demo filing in under 15 minutes with no credit card required. Click "Try the Demo Free" to launch an interactive demo with your vessel data, see how the AI validation works, and receive a sample compliance score. Does CanalClear integrate with my existing TMS?+
Yes. CanalClear imports vessel particulars from Veson IMOS, Q88, Dataloy, ShipNet, and other fleet management systems via API or CSV. We also support SAML 2.0 and OIDC SSO (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace) so your team keeps existing credentials. An open REST API is available for custom integrations.
