What is CanalClear?

CanalClear is an AI-powered maritime compliance automation platform that handles VUMPA filings (Panama), SCA transit permits (Suez), SP-1 pre-arrival notifications (Bosporus/Turkish Straits), STRAITREP filings (Malacca), and ISPS pre-arrival notifications (Cape of Good Hope) for fleet operators, ship agents, and charterers. It replaces expensive ship agent fees ($2,000–$5,000+ per transit) by automating the entire compliance documentation workflow across the world's five critical maritime chokepoints.

Coverage includes Panama Canal (VUMPA, PCSOPEP, crew manifests), Suez Canal (SCA forms, SCNT tonnage, ISPS notifications, convoy booking), Bosporus/Turkish Straits (SP-1 filing, Montreux Convention checks, DG notifications), Malacca Strait (STRAITREP, VTIS compliance, MARPOL validation), and Cape of Good Hope (SAMSA checks, ISPS filings, 96h pre-arrival validation). CanalClear automates all five workflows — enter vessel data once, get compliant filings for whichever waterway you're transiting.

Available at canalclear.org. Five-waterway bundle from $2,799/month (Agent Pro) or $1,499/vessel/month (Ops Manager) — save 20% vs individual plans.
