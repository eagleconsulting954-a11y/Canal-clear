Methodology
  • Suez: 2024 SCA SCNT-based tariff from suez_fee_tiers table + approximate SCA ancillary charges
  • Panama: ACP 2024 PC/UMS-based tariff — PC/UMS estimated as 1.08 × GT
  • Both estimates use laden or ballast rates as selected
  • Panama transit is ~8–10h; Suez is ~14–16h. Factor in fuel burn differences for full cost comparison
  • Route via Cape of Good Hope adds ~10 days and ~$500K fuel for a VLCC — factoring this in favors both canals
⚠ Estimates Only. These figures are for planning purposes. Actual SCA invoices depend on: certified SCNT certificates, current IMF SDR/USD rate on invoice date, pilotage zone assignments, vessel LOA surcharges, and rebate pre-approval. Always confirm fees with your SCA agent before transit.
