Suez Canal SCA Filing Deadline Cheat Sheet

1
SCA Transit Application (TA)
72 hours before entering the Canal
⚠ ETA entered in local time instead of UTC
2
Crew List (FAL 5)
48 hours before arrival at anchorage
⚠ Missing STCW certificate endorsement numbers
3
Cargo Declaration (FAL 2 / IMDG)
48 hours before arrival
⚠ DG entries missing UN number or stow location
4
ISPS Security Declaration (ISSC)
24 hours before arrival
⚠ Expired ISSC or wrong security level declared
5
SOPEP Summary
24 hours before arrival
⚠ References superseded MEPC resolution
6
Ballast Water Management Declaration
24 hours before arrival
⚠ Exchange records missing GPS coordinates
7
Tonnage Certificate Summary (SCNT)
With Transit Application (72h)
⚠ SCNT figures don't match ITC 69
