Methodology & Data Sources

Rejection rates: Manual filing 3.4% · Third-party agent 1.8% · In-house team 2.2% · Automated system (CanalClear) 0.4%. Based on Panama Canal Authority rejection data, SCA customs agency reports, and CanalClear's 2024–2025 processing records.

Cost per rejection event: $65,000 baseline, adjusted ×1.0–1.8 by cargo type (LNG highest: ×1.8). Includes vessel delay at prevailing time-charter rates, port authority re-submission fees, agent surcharges, and schedule slip cascades to downstream ports.

Failure category split: VUMPA errors 32% · Customs mismatches 28% · PCSOPEP gaps 24% · Schedule slip cascades 16%. Derived from Panama Canal Authority rejection log analysis (n=2,100 rejections, 2023–2024).

Confidence range: ±25% around point estimate. Actual exposure varies with flag state, vessel age, transit complexity, and seasonal traffic patterns.
