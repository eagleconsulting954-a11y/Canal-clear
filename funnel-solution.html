The Process

Three steps. Zero filing errors.

1

Upload Your Documents

Drag and drop your vessel documents — crew manifests, cargo declarations, equipment certificates, PCSOPEP files. CanalClear's AI extracts and structures the data automatically. Supports PDF, Word, and image formats.

  • AI reads handwritten and scanned documents
  • Auto-extracts crew names, IMO numbers, cert dates
  • Supports 5 languages — EN, ES, ZH, EL, JA
📂
2

AI Validates Against ACP Requirements

The compliance engine runs the 23-point validation engine against your filing package in real time. Every error is flagged with the exact requirement it violates, the fix required, and the regulatory reference. No guessing.

  • Checks all 3 government agency requirements simultaneously
  • Catches 94% of errors before you submit
  • Instant fix suggestions with regulatory citations
🤖
3

One-Click Submit & Download

Once validated, submit your VUMPA package directly or export audit-ready PDFs. Your filing history is stored, certificates are tracked, and you receive alerts before any documentation expires.

  • Direct ACP portal submission (Pro+ plans)
  • Audit-ready PDF export for every filing
  • Certificate expiry alerts — never miss a renewal
