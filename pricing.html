Agency Questions

How does an Agency plan differ from the fleet operator plans?
Agency plans are built for shipping agencies managing multiple client vessels across all 5 waterways — Panama, Suez, Bosporus, Malacca, and Cape of Good Hope — under one flat monthly fee. Fleet operator plans (Ops Manager) are per-vessel/month for owner-operators managing their own fleet. Agency plans include a multi-client workspace, batch filing, and higher vessel caps.
What counts as an "active vessel"?
Any vessel with at least one filing or compliance record in the billing period. Archive a vessel when it's not actively transiting and it won't count against your cap.
Can I upgrade between agency tiers?
Yes. Upgrade anytime — the new rate applies at your next billing date. You won't lose any data or filings.
Do agency plans replace the need for individual waterway subscriptions?
Yes. All agency plans include all 5 waterways — Panama (VUMPA/PCSOPEP), Suez (SCA/SCNT/ISPS), Bosporus (SP-1/Montreux), Malacca (STRAITREP/VTIS), and Cape of Good Hope (ISPS pre-arrival). You get full access across every route for one flat rate.

Common Questions

What's the difference between the Agency plans and Ops Manager?
Agency plans are for shipping agents managing client filings — flat monthly rates ($999–$2,999/mo) across all 5 waterways with multi-client workspace and batch filing. The Ops Manager is for fleet operators — it continuously tracks PCSOPEP, SCA, and SP-1 compliance per vessel ($399–$599/vessel/mo) and protects against $65K+ fines.
Why is Suez Canal priced higher than Panama and Bosporus?
Suez Canal has 12+ mandatory filings per transit versus Panama's 3–4 and Bosporus's 2–3. SCA compliance requires SCNT tonnage certificates, ISPS security declarations, convoy booking coordination, and 26 vessel dimension rules — each enforced by SCA Maritime. The complexity warrants the premium.
What does the Bosporus SP-1 plan cover?
The Bosporus plans cover Turkish Straits SP-1 pre-transit notifications, Montreux Convention compliance, >200m LOA daylight scheduling alerts, and deadline tracking. Agency plans handle unlimited SP-1 filings across all waterways; Ops Manager manages fleet-wide SP-1 compliance and batch filing per vessel. Learn more at /bosporus.
How much do I save with the All Waterways Bundle?
The Ops Manager bundle is $1,299/vessel/mo vs. $2,295 separately ($399 Panama + $599 Suez + $499 Bosporus + $449 Malacca + $349 Cape) — saving $996/vessel/mo. If you operate across all 5 waterways, the bundle is the clear choice. Agency plans already include all 5 waterways at a flat rate.
Why flat monthly pricing instead of per-transit fees?
Per-transit pricing creates friction every time you file. A flat monthly rate means you can file as often as needed without second-guessing the cost. Traditional ship agents charge $2,000–$5,000+ per transit — CanalClear's Ops Manager pays for itself within the first month of compliance tracking, and Agency plans cover unlimited filings across all waterways.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. All plans are billed monthly with no minimum term. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
Can I see the product before paying?
Yes — try the VUMPA validator demo at no cost, no login required. Use the "get a free compliance assessment" link under any plan to have our team walk you through the full workflow before you commit.
