Role Reference

What each role can and cannot do.

Admin

Full access: manage users, billing, SSO, all waterways, all filings, all settings.

Compliance Officer

Create, edit, and submit filings. Manage alerts. Export reports. Cannot manage users or billing.

Operator

Create and edit draft filings only. Cannot submit. Read-only fleet dashboard.

Viewer

Read-only access to filings, reports, and fleet dashboard. No edits or submissions.
