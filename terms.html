1 Service Description

CanalClear ("Service," "platform," "we," "us") is a maritime compliance software-as-a-service that automates the preparation, validation, and documentation of transit filings for:

  • Panama Canal (ACP): VUMPA (Vessel Universal Measurement and Pre-Arrival) filings, PCSOPEP (Panama Canal Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan) documentation, ACP Maritime Service Portal submissions
  • Suez Canal (SCA/SCNT): Transit Requirement Sheets, ISPS notifications, tonnage certificates, convoy scheduling, SCA submission packages
  • Bosporus Strait (SP-1): SP-1 Peril Statement documentation and Bosporus compliance filing support

CanalClear provides software tools to assist maritime professionals in preparing compliant documentation. It is not a licensed ship agent, and is not a party to any filing relationship between you and the relevant canal authority (ACP, SCA, or Turkish Straits).
