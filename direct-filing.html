Why Direct Filing

The manual portal workflow is the last mile problem

CanalClear already validates and prepares every filing. The only remaining step is walking to the window and handing it over. Direct filing automates that step.

🚫
No more portal gymnastics

Today: download the validated PDF from CanalClear, log into the authority portal, navigate to the right section, upload, submit. With direct filing: click Send. That's it. The rest is handled.

📡
Real-time submission status

Authority portals rarely confirm receipt immediately. Direct integration polls the authority system and surfaces status in CanalClear — accepted, pending, or rejected — so you're not checking your inbox and hoping.

🔄
Automatic rejection handling

Rejections happen. An ISPS level field missing, a tonnage figure in the wrong format. Direct filing parses the rejection reason, corrects the issue, and resubmits — all before you've had your morning coffee.

💸
No extra cost vs. doing it yourself

Unlike a ship agent who charges $2,000–$5,000 per transit just to handle paperwork, direct filing is included in your CanalClear plan. Same monthly rate, zero manual intervention required.

⏰
Deadline-aware auto-submission

Every authority has a filing window — 96h for Panama, 72h for Suez, 48h for Turkish VTS dangerous cargo. Direct filing submits at the optimal time within each window, not at the last minute.

🗂️
Complete audit trail

Every submission, acknowledgement, rejection, and resubmission is logged with timestamps. If a PSC inspector asks for filing history, you export it in 30 seconds — no more email archaeology.
