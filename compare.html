Frequently Asked Questions

Do I still need a ship agent with CanalClear?

Most fleet operators find CanalClear replaces 80–90% of what a ship agent does for compliance filing. Ship agents are still useful for in-port services — tugs, pilots, line handlers, fresh water, stores. But for VUMPA preparation, PCSOPEP management, and pre-transit document validation, CanalClear handles it fully. Some operators use CanalClear alongside their existing agent; others have reduced or eliminated their agent relationship for transit compliance.

How does CanalClear handle VUMPA filings?

You enter your vessel IMO number and voyage details — CanalClear's AI pre-fills known vessel data from maritime registries. You then upload or enter your cargo manifest, crew credentials, and PCSOPEP. CanalClear validates every field against ACP 2026 portal requirements, flags errors in plain language with specific fixes, and generates a complete submission package ready for ACP upload. The entire process takes under 10 minutes per transit.

What if CanalClear flags an error in my filing?

CanalClear tells you exactly what the error is, which field it's in, and how to fix it — with specific corrections, not generic instructions. For example: 'PCSOPEP Section 4.3: Emergency contacts must include a Panamanian port authority number in +507 format.' You fix the error in CanalClear and re-validate before submission. This prevents the $15,000+ VUMPA rejection fine entirely.

Can ship agents use CanalClear?

Yes — and it changes their economics significantly. A ship agent handling 50+ transits per year can use CanalClear to serve their own clients faster and with fewer errors. The Agent Pro plan starts at $599/month for Panama and $899/month for Suez (or $1,500/month for both canals), with unlimited filings and bulk workflows designed for agents managing multiple client fleets. CanalClear turns a 2-hour manual process per filing into a 5-minute automated one.
