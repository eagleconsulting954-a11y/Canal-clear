What is SCNT tonnage? SCNT (Suez Canal Net Tonnage) is the Suez Canal Authority's proprietary tonnage measurement system, derived from the 1873 Moorsom method, used to calculate transit fees. SCNT typically differs from a vessel's official IMO net tonnage by 5–15%. All Suez Canal transit fees are based on SCNT, not gross or net tonnage per LOADLINE convention.

How long does Suez Canal filing take? Suez Canal filing involves three sequential deadlines: ISPS security declarations at 72h, 48h, and 24h before arrival; the full SCA transit application lodged 24–48h before transit; and IMDG dangerous cargo notifications requiring additional lead time. FAQ

Common Suez Compliance Questions

What documents are required for Suez Canal transit?
The SCA requires at minimum: SCA Transit Application, Crew Manifest, Cargo Declaration (IMDG 42-24 compliant), ISPS Security Declaration (72h/48h/24h pre-arrival), SOPEP Summary, Ballast Water Management Declaration, and Tonnage Certificate (SCNT). CanalClear automates all of these in a single workflow.
Why do 30–40% of first Suez transits fail compliance checks?
Most failures come from three sources: missed ISPS notification timing (filing too late or with incomplete port-call history), SCNT calculation errors (confusing Suez Canal Net Tonnage with gross tonnage), and IMDG cargo classification mistakes updated in Amendment 42-24. CanalClear's 26-rule validation engine catches all of these before you submit.
How does IMDG Amendment 42-24 affect Suez Canal filings?
Amendment 42-24 revised classification rules for lithium batteries — UN3480, UN3481, UN3090, UN3091. The SCA now enforces these at the point of transit. CanalClear validates cargo declarations against the latest IMDG standards and flags non-compliant items automatically before you file.

How are Suez Canal tolls calculated, and can I reduce them?
Tolls are based on Suez Canal Net Tonnage (SCNT), not gross tonnage. The SCA applies a tiered tariff to SCNT, with rebates up to 75% for qualifying ballast voyages and bulk carriers.
